A live mirror of the road

The map shows the road itself — the same junctions, lanes and markings you see through the glass — so you know where to go without decoding a flat arrow.

Everything around you, live

Surrounding vehicles, pedestrians, roadworks and lane closures appear in their real positions as they happen — the same awareness your driver-assist systems have, shown on the map.

Precise to your exact lane

The map knows the specific lane you're in — so guidance can point to a lane, not just a direction.

See exactly where you are — and what's around you

Where driver-assist data is available, the live 3D view fills in the world moving around you.

Positioned to the lane

The vehicle sits in the lane it's actually in, not somewhere on the road's centreline.

Using the car's own eyes

Positioning comes from the vehicle's existing cameras matched against map reference features. No extra sensor hardware.

The traffic around you, on the map

Surrounding vehicles appear where they actually are, moving as they actually move.

Things that weren't there yesterday

Cones, pylons, a closed lane. The transient world no survey pass can capture.

Guidance that knows which lane you're in

Positioning precise enough to place your car in the right lane — and keep guidance clear right when it matters.

Detail where it counts

The experience concentrates precision in the zone the driver is about to drive through.

Which lane, and when

Guidance resolves to a specific lane at a specific moment, not a direction and a hope.

Held, and moved over

Active lane guidance keeps the driver in the right lane; lane-change assistance moves them across when the route needs it.

Mapbox continuously improves underlying map data. Over-the-air updates are separate from application updates.

Any screen. Or all of them.

The Mapbox Navigation SDK provides everything needed to build a fully integrated in-vehicle navigation experience, including maps, routing, turn-by-turn guidance, and UI components. It also supports advanced features like live traffic, lane guidance, and offline navigation — without requiring teams to maintain underlying data or algorithms.

The Mapbox Navigation SDK is highly modular and customizable, allowing automakers to design branded, differentiated navigation experiences. Teams can tailor everything from map styling and UI components to interaction models.

Map updates are sent as incremental "delta" updates over the air. Instead of downloading entire map regions, the SDK only fetches map tiles or specific attribute changes, keeping the onboard map current with minimal bandwidth consumption.

Mapbox Navigation SDK features a hybrid-first architecture, using local, onboard map data for routing and guidance when offline, then seamlessly synchronizing with cloud services once a connection is restored.
